ZF Prometo corporate font binaries are intentionally not distributed in this ZIP.

If authorised by your organisation, put these TWO web fonts from your own approved asset source into this folder:
  ZFPrometo_W_Lt.woff2
  ZFPrometo_W_Md.woff2

Your included src/brand-fonts.css already refers to those paths.
Without them, the application remains functional and falls back to Arial/system fonts.
Do not publish brand assets unless approved for public use.
